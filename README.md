# Intelligent Predictive Analytics Platform

A reusable machine-learning platform containing independent,
dataset-specific predictive analytics applications.

## Applications

1. MetroPT Industrial AI
   Industrial equipment intelligence and predictive maintenance.

2. NASA Space AI
   Atmospheric and environmental predictive analytics using
   NASA MERRA-2 reanalysis data.

3. Appliances Energy AI
   Energy consumption forecasting and chronological validation.

## Shared Capabilities

- Predictive modelling
- Uncertainty estimation
- Anomaly detection
- Data drift monitoring
- Cost-sensitive adaptive retraining
- Model evaluation and explainability
- Interactive what-if analysis

## Development Principle

Each dataset has its own independent data processing,
training and evaluation pipeline. Unrelated datasets are
never combined into one training dataset.
